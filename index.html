Process

From research to iteration

The format is never the strategy by itself. It is the vehicle for a specific message, and every step below exists so the next test is better than the last one.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Product, customer, offer, margins, markets, brand voice, funnel stage and previous winners and losers.

  2. 02

    Research

    Customer language, reviews, competitor ads, Meta Ad Library, category patterns, objections and recurring situations.

  3. 03

    Hypotheses

    Turn research into specific things worth testing rather than a list of random formats.

  4. 04

    Concept banks

    One concept and format, with around five hook and copy variations built on the same core idea where the format supports it.

  5. 05

    Briefs

    Audience, funnel stage, message, hook variants, copy, visual logic, prompts or shot direction, safe zones and market adaptations.

  6. 06

    Execution + QA

    Designers, editors and UGC creators execute. I review against the strategic idea and give specific edit notes.

  7. 07

    Meta analysis

    Read spend, CTR, CPC, CPM, CVR and ROAS in Ads Manager across the whole bank, not ad by ad.

  8. 08

    Iterate

    Expand patterns that repeat, rework sound angles with weak execution, and move on when the hypothesis does not hold.

Testing methodology

Concept first, then controlled variation

I do not judge a whole concept from one ad.

One weak hook can make a strong concept look bad. One unusually strong hook can make an average execution look better than it really is.

So each concept becomes a small creative bank: around five hook and copy variations while the core angle and visual logic stay consistent. That gives enough controlled variation to separate whether performance comes from the angle, the visual execution, or the copy.

It also gives Meta several executions of the same idea, so different audience segments can each find the version that resonates with them.

  • Repeatable winnerSeveral hooks on the same concept perform. Stronger signal, so I expand the bank.
  • Single-version winnerOnly one variation works. The angle is not validated yet, so I build more controlled variations to understand why.
  • Attention problemConverts on site but CTR is weak. I test hooks, openings and visual execution.
  • Conversion problemEarns clicks but CVR or ROAS is weak. I inspect message-to-offer alignment, objections and landing-page intent.
  • No signalNothing improves after meaningful spend. I move to the next hypothesis instead of polishing endlessly.
